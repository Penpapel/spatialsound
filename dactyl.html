DACTYL

Version 3.1

A synthesizer you play with your fingers. Every gesture shapes the sound.

Gestures

  • Drag

    Left and right sets the pitch. Up and down opens the filter.

  • Scrub

    Move fast to push the filter into resonance.

  • Hold

    Keep still for half a second to latch a drone. Tap the ring to let one go, or double tap the field to release every drone at once.

  • Flick

    Lift your finger while moving to throw the sound into the echo.

  • Two fingers

    Spread to widen the unison. Twist to morph the waveform from sine to square.

Compose

  • Layers

    Each circle at the top is a layer with its own sound. Tap one to play it, tap it again to turn it off. Hold to switch it without changing layers. Plus adds a layer, up to six.

  • Loop

    Press Rec, play, press Stop. Your gestures repeat on that layer. Later layers record in time with the first loop. Open Patch to clear a loop or delete a layer.

  • Wheels

    Sound, FX, Tempo, Quant and Visual at the bottom work like the iPhone timer. Tap to switch on or off. Press or drag to scroll through the values. All but Tempo belong to the selected layer. At the end of the FX wheel are the Arp rows: held and looped notes step through the scale in time. Patch → Space has every Arp control.

  • Click and tempo

    Tap Tempo to switch the click on, scroll it to set the BPM. Rec then counts you in and trims your first loop to whole bars. Once loops exist, scrolling Tempo retimes all of them together and the pitch stays.

  • Quantize

    Scroll Quant to pick 1/4, 1/8 or 1/16 and snap the selected loop to the grid. Tap it again to hear your original timing.

  • Visuals

    Each layer has its own visual. Scroll Visual, or swipe a layer circle up or down. In Patch, Visuals can hide the interface; the UI button or Escape brings it back.

  • Geyser demo

    Writes a piece on six layers: a long quiet, then a burst of steam and noise. The parts come in and out and the piece keeps changing. Play over it, or record your own loops on top. Press it again for a new take.

  • Evolve

    Once you have a loop, the ∞ button at the top evolves what you made. Every cycle a layer comes in or drops out, answer layers appear, the sound drifts and the key sometimes moves. Tap a layer to take it over. Tap ∞ again to put everything back.

  • Tour

    Take the tour below for an automatic walk through the gestures. Touch the screen to stop it.

Export

  • Sound

    Open Patch and scroll to Export. Bounce loop saves a seamless WAV of 1 to 8 cycles, Record take saves whatever you play, MIDI saves the loops as notes, and Save composition keeps your layers, loops and settings in a small file you can open again with Open composition. The pause button at the right edge of the loop line stops and starts everything.

  • Video

    Bounce video records the field and the sound together for the cycles you choose. Video take records whatever you play. With Clean video on, the picture is the visuals only: no gesture lines, no interface.

Orchestrate

  • Rig

    Open Patch and go to Room. Start a rig and invite friends on the same Wi-Fi with a QR code, a WhatsApp link or Share. Their phones become speakers and the sound moves between them. Drag layers and phones around the room map. Calibrate measures each phone's delay with your microphone, and Sync test plays a chirp to check by ear.

The Geyser demo replaces your current layers and loops. Press it again for a new take.

No sound? Switch off the silent mode switch on the side of your iPhone and raise the volume.
